## 1. Project Overview & One-Line Pitch

**Lagos On Chain (LOC)** brings Lagos youth culture, street food discovery, and underground nightlife directly onto Solana through **Solana Actions & Blinks** for 1-click social ticketing and **Proof-of-Physical-Vibe (PoPV)** compressed NFT check-ins.

---

## 2. The Problem: The High-Friction Reality of Lagos

Nigeria is the **#2 crypto market globally**, with millions of youth using stablecoins and wallets daily. Yet, their physical lifestyle remains chained to a failing domestic banking rail:
1. **POS Failure at Gate Entrances**: At late-night concerts, raves, and pop-ups (Street Souk, Mainland BlockParty), point-of-sale network failure rates hover between **20% and 35%**, stranding attendees at gates and causing massive revenue loss for creators.
2. **Aggressive Ticket Scalping & Fake Passes**: Conventional paper and barcode ticketing platforms in Nigeria are plagued by duplicate screenshots and black-market markup.
3. **The Data Burden of Heavy Web3 Apps**: Nigerian youth resent downloading 120MB native apps that consume expensive mobile data bundles.
4. **Inflation & High Merchant POS Charges**: Traditional bank gateway charges (1.5% + stamp duty + transfer fees) squeeze indie food spots operating on tight margins.

---

## 3. The Solution: Solana-Native Consumer Lifestyle OS

Lagos On Chain provides an ultra-lightweight (<5MB), mobile-first Progressive Web App paired with **Solana Actions (Blinks)**:

* **1-Click Social Ticketing (Solana Blinks)**: Event creators paste a Dialect Blink link directly into a tweet on X (Twitter) or WhatsApp group. Attendees buy anti-scalping tickets in 1 second using Phantom or Solflare with sub-cent gas fees.
* **Proof-of-Physical-Vibe (PoPV)**: Rather than vulnerable GPS tracking (which is easily spoofed from university dorm rooms), LOC uses **dynamic NFC & rotating QR pucks** placed at partner venues. Tapping a puck validates physical proximity and mints a zero-gas compressed NFT (cNFT) to the user's Explorer Passport.
* **Under ₦10,000 Food Radar**: Curating the authentic culinary staples of Lagos youth (Korede Spaghetti Akoka, Glover Court Suya, Amala Shitta) with dual Naira/Solana pricing and on-chain loyalty perks.
* **Automated 3.5% Sustainable Platform Cut**: Every ticket transaction automatically splits 3.5% into the platform treasury, generating immediate self-sustaining cash flow from Day 1.
